### Research

- **[LeNAHS](https://github.com/KadirKess/LeNAHS)** (Learning Noise-Aware Hierarchical Structures): ongoing research at LRE. A recurrent graph neural network recovers a hierarchical structure close to clean image's from its noisy version. PyTorch Geometric, Higra.

### Personal research projects

- **[CAGE](https://github.com/KadirKess/CAGE)** (Classifier-Aware Generative Enhancement): a U-Net denoiser trained against a frozen classifier. Its outputs reach 0.69 accuracy against 0.57 on the clean images (3 seeds), and Grad-CAM shows the class-discriminative patterns it adds beyond denoising.
- **[KHUNC](https://github.com/KadirKess/KHUNC)** (Kinda Half U-Net Compression): image compression with a split U-Net, evaluated with SSIMULACRA2 on the 24 Kodak images.

### Image synthesis

- **[SingularityRenderer](https://github.com/KadirKess/SingularityRenderer)**: physically based rendering of a rotating (Kerr) black hole and its accretion disk, with RK4 geodesic integration on CPU and CUDA. With Valentin Best.
- **[ZeFauxRest](https://github.com/KadirKess/ZeFauxRest)**: real-time volumetric forest in C++17 and OpenGL 4.5, with an 8-pass deferred pipeline, ray-marched clouds and godrays. With Martin Boulanger.

### Other projects

- **[OMaR](https://github.com/KadirKess/OMaR)** (Optical M(a)usic Recognition): score transcription with no trained classifier, using directional morphology and graph rewriting to go from printed scores to MIDI. 0.51 mAP on DeepScoresV2, and the C++20 port is 6.1x faster than the Python reference. With Sacha Melin.
- **[Slopetimization](https://github.com/KadirKess/Slopetimization)**: comparison of gradient descent, conjugate gradient, Newton and BFGS on convex and non-convex functions. With Sacha Melin and Valentin Best.
- **[Soratra](https://github.com/KadirKess/Soratra)**: a self-hosted reading journal for a small circle of friends.
